About the Game

What is Color Catcher?

Color Catcher is a fun, fast-paced catching game for kids ages 4 and up. Players guide a basket to catch falling stars, gems, treats, and surprises while dodging skulls, spiders, and bats that appear in later levels.


In a children's app market dominated by free-to-play titles monetized through advertising and data collection, Color Catcher takes a fundamentally different approach: no ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, and full offline functionality.

Key Features

What makes it different

  • Zero ads — no banners, no video ads, no sponsored content
  • Zero data collection — fully COPPA compliant
  • Zero in-app purchases — one price, everything included
  • Works 100% offline — no internet required
  • Three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
  • Power-ups: wider basket, slow motion, shields
  • Combo scoring system with multipliers
  • Milestone celebrations with fireworks
  • Local leaderboard with avatar selection
  • Changing sky backgrounds as you level up
